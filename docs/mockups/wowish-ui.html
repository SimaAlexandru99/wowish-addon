Wowish looks at your character when you are out of combat and tells you the few things worth doing next: quests ready to hand in, unspent talent points, full bags, where to quest, and (for Hunters) ammo.
Never acts in combat. Never presses a button or casts anything for you. Waypoints only when you click one.
